About NotesLab

Empowering Engineers with Knowledge.

Our Story

Hey there! We are a group of engineering students who realized how scattered and confusing study materials can be. NotesLab is our attempt to fix that. We've compiled the best notes we used to ace our exams, so you can too. It's built by students, for students.

What's Inside?

  • Handrowritten notes that actually make sense.
  • Last minute revision guides (our secret weapon).
  • PYQs sorted by unit (because who has time to search?).
  • Updated syllabus decodes.
